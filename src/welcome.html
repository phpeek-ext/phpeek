Welcome to PHPeek

A safety net for hacked websites. Here's exactly what it does, what it can see, and what it never does.

👀 PHP2Shell check

Reads the response headers of the page you open and shows a red ! when the server advertises a PHP version affected by PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619). It's what the server reports, not proof either way.

Sees: response headers of the page you open. Changes: nothing.

💳 Card Guard

When you type a card number, PHPeek remembers it in that page only and blocks a page script from sending it to a site that is neither the store nor a known payment provider. That's how card skimmers on hacked stores work. On stores that look exposed, you get a heads-up at the card field.

Sees: numbers typed into page fields and requests the page sends while a card is typed. Never stores or sends your card anywhere.

🔑 Password Guard

On sites that look exposed to PHP2Shell, a heads-up when you reach a password field. It never reads your password.

🛡️ Seed Shield · ✍️ Wallet signature check · 📋 Clipboard Guard

  • Seed Shield: a crypto recovery phrase typed or pasted into a website is cleared, and you're told why.
  • Wallet signature check: before your wallet opens, risky requests (unlimited token approvals, "approve all NFTs", Permit, blind signing) are paused and explained. You decide. PHPeek never sees keys and never signs anything.
  • Clipboard Guard: if a page secretly puts a command in your clipboard (fake "I'm not a robot" pages), it's replaced with a harmless warning.

Sees: text typed into page fields, wallet requests the page makes, text the page writes to your clipboard. Nothing is stored or sent.

🔎 Check any site

Type an address in the PHPeek popup to check it before you open it. This is the only time PHPeek contacts a website: it loads that one page, without cookies, and reads its headers.

Never

  • No tracking, analytics or ads
  • No PHPeek server, no remote code
  • Never sends your data anywhere

Honest limits

  • It can't remove malware already on your computer
  • Some skimmers and drainers can still get through
  • Prefer PayPal / Apple Pay / Google Pay on unknown stores

Warnings open in PHPeek's own window, so a website can't hide or fake them. Open source: github.com/phpeek-ext/phpeek · Privacy policy
